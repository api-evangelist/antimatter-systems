# Antimatter Systems

## Products

- [Antimatter (web app)](https://playantimatter.com): The learning platform where students create memes, puzzles and games to show subject comprehension.
- [Sorcerer](https://playantimatter.com/sorcerer): AI instructional coach.
- [Antimatter for Educators](https://antimatter.systems/foreducators): Teacher-facing overview — activity deadlines and instructions, shareable student invite links, submission moderation and view-only mode, pre-approved template library, plagiarism detection on unedited image uploads, and centralized grading.
- [Antimatter Classroom](https://antimatter.systems/classroom): Classroom product overview.
- [Antimatter Certifications](https://antimatter.systems/certifications): Educator certification program.
- [Professional Development](https://antimatter.systems/pd): PD offering for educators.
- Antimatter: Creator and Antimatter: Universe — consumer iOS apps.

## Disambiguation

Search results for "Antimatter API" almost always refer to that other company, or to the unrelated *Antimatter Dimensions* game and *Antimatter API* Minecraft mod.
